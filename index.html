Untangling the probate you inherited.
Planning to avoid the one you’d leave.

Florida probate is complicated. Homestead restrictions, clouded title, and outdated deeds can slow the sale or transfer of what is often the estate’s most valuable asset: the real estate itself. We help families work through probate when it happens, and we help clients plan their estates with wills, trusts, and deeds to avoid probate where possible.

How We Can Help

Probate Administration

Guiding families through Florida probate, from opening the estate to final distribution.

Lady Bird Deeds

Enhanced life estate deeds that let you keep full control of your Florida home during your lifetime and are designed to pass it to your chosen beneficiaries outside of probate.

Real Property in Estates and Trusts

Homestead questions, deeds, title issues, and the sale or transfer of real estate.

Estate Planning

Wills, trusts, and deeds that account for Florida homestead law, with trusts and deeds used to avoid probate where possible.
